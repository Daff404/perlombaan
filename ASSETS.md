# SHION original asset pack

Five original raster images were made using the built-in ImageGen tool, then copied into `assets/images/`. They are bundled in the app; decorative images do not require a network connection.

| File | Prompt / creative specification |
| --- | --- |
| forest.png | Vertical editorial macro photography of glossy tropical leaves, small water droplets, forest greens and sage highlights, dark central negative space, no text or interface. |
| recycling.png | Reuse still life: PET bottle, clean folded cardboard, sage fabric tote, young seedling and green recycling container, tropical foliage, warm diffused daylight, no labels or interface. |
| transport.png | Contemporary green city bus and bicycle on a peaceful tree-lined Indonesian street, dappled morning light, emerald and sage palette, no brands or lettering. |
| organic.png | Vegetable peels and dry leaves in a compost bowl beside a young seedling, mossy garden bench, tropical greenery, natural morning light, no writing. |
| reuse.png | Sage reusable metal bottle, glass lunch container, cotton tote and leafy plant by a naturally lit window, forest green counter, premium editorial still life, no brands. |

The 15 SVG files in `assets/icons/` are original code-authored vector assets: brand mark, leaf, home, scan, bag, book, pin, profile, camera, recycle, gift, carbon, plastic, organic and paper. Primary navigation and dashboard shortcuts use these files. Standard platform action glyphs remain Material Icons, supplied by Flutter.

Three bundled font families:
- Cormorant Garamond: serif headings.
- Manrope: body text and controls.
- Fraunces: balance and metric numerals.

Font files and SIL Open Font License texts are in `assets/fonts/`. Sources: `https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond`, `/manrope`, `/fraunces`.

Artwork is illustrative. It is not used as evidence of a real deposit, verified partner, or redeemable transport voucher.
