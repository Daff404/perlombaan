The 15 SVG files in `assets/icons/` are original code-authored vector assets: brand mark, leaf, home, scan, bag, book, pin, profile, camera, recycle, gift, carbon, plastic, organic and paper. The brand mark is reused in the animated SHION startup splash and is also present in Android launcher resources. Primary navigation and dashboard shortcuts use these files. Standard platform action glyphs remain Material Icons, supplied by Flutter.

Three bundled font families:
- Cormorant Garamond: serif headings.
- Manrope: body text and controls.
- Fraunces: balance and metric numerals.

Font files and SIL Open Font License texts are in `assets/fonts/`. Sources: `https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond`, `/manrope`, `/fraunces`.

Artwork is illustrative. It is not used as evidence of a real deposit, verified partner, or redeemable transport voucher.
