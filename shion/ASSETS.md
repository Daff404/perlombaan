The 16 SVG files in `assets/icons/` are original code-authored vector assets: brand mark, leaf, home, scan, bag, book, pin, profile, camera, recycle, gift, carbon, plastic, organic, paper, and sorting scene. The sorting scene is a custom vector illustration of separated material bins and new growth, used on the home scan action. The brand mark is reused in the animated SHION startup splash and is also present in Android launcher resources. Primary navigation and dashboard shortcuts use these files. Standard platform action glyphs remain Material Icons, supplied by Flutter.

`assets/vector/` contains twelve original SHION eco illustrations as transparent PNGs, prepared for direct use in Flutter. Six are assigned to distinct onboarding and account steps: greeting, AI scan introduction, login, registration, password setup, and account-created welcome. Six more depict waste sorting, AI scanning, planting, reusable living, a community waste bank, and eco education for the main app. Their leafy halos, sun shapes, dotted trails, and other botanical ornaments are part of each scene. The artwork was created specifically for SHION and does not use an icon or illustration library.

Three bundled font families:
- Cormorant Garamond: serif headings.
- Manrope: body text and controls.
- Fraunces: balance and metric numerals.

Font files and SIL Open Font License texts are in `assets/fonts/`. Sources: `https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond`, `/manrope`, `/fraunces`.

Artwork is illustrative. It is not used as evidence of a real deposit, verified partner, or redeemable transport voucher.
